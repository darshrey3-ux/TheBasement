The Basement is the game studio of Darshrey Studio. Down here there are no windows, no trends to chase and no rules about what a game should be. Just small teams, loud ideas and way too much coffee.

Every game gets the Darshrey treatment: bold art direction, motion that feels alive and a launch people talk about. We make worlds you want to get lost in.
